About JSON Studio

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Parsing, formatting and conversion all happen locally in your browser.

What can JSON Studio do?

Format, minify, validate with exact error positions, auto-repair, sort keys, flatten, explore as a tree or table, query with JSONPath, compare two documents, validate against a JSON Schema, and convert to YAML, XML, CSV or TypeScript.

How do I copy a path?

Click any key in the tree view.
